# FIN-006 — Transactions page: Actual and Planned tabs

Status: IMPLEMENTED — automated validation passed; phone acceptance pending

## UX problem

FIN-004 and FIN-005 added recurring occurrences and one-off planned expenses to the
Transactions page. The page stacked them in one long scroll: a Planned heading, a
Recurring group, a One-off group, and only then Actual transactions. In practice:

- real history was pushed below the fold by planning that has no accounting effect;
- planned and actual items were visually similar, so it was unclear what had
  really happened versus what was only expected;
- the two planning kinds used different status wording (recurring showed the raw
  `Projected`, one-off showed `Expected`) and did not say which actions applied;
- management pages (Recurring, Planned expenses) were plain trailing links.

## Scope

App presentation only. No Domain, Application, API, contract or persistence change;
**no migration**. FIN-004 and FIN-005 behavior (status derivation, confirm/skip/
cancel rules, review fields, idempotent retries, budget formulas) is unchanged and
reuses the existing action components and flows. Home, Portfolio, the shell, the
bottom dock and FinanceHub are untouched.

## Choices

- **Two tabs, one month.** A segmented control (existing `lo-segmented` style)
  switches between **Actual** and **Planned**. The month selector stays above the
  tabs and applies to both, so switching tabs never changes the month.
- **Actual is the default.** Opening Transactions (dock, Home "View all", Finance
  hub) lands on real history, as before FIN-004.
- **Tab in the query string.** `?tab=planned` opens Planned; any other value opens
  Actual. Switching tabs replaces the history entry, so Back still leaves the page
  and the same component instance keeps the selected month and loaded data.
- **Load once, switch instantly.** Month navigation and action completion refresh
  history and both planning kinds together (unchanged from FIN-004/005), because a
  confirmation creates an actual transaction and removes the plan. Tab switches
  make no request.
- **Due count on the tab.** The Planned tab reads `Planned · N due` when the month
  has Due items, so pending confirmations are visible from Actual.
- **One status wording.** Both kinds show `Due` or `Expected` (Projected) as a pill;
  Due is emphasised.
- **Pure helper.** `PlannedMonthView` (App service) builds the month's unresolved
  recurring and one-off lists from the existing `RecurringPlanning` and
  `PlannedExpensePlanning` filters, plus the due count and labels, so the page logic
  is unit-testable without a UI test framework.

## Tab behavior

### Actual

Only real Transactions for the selected local month, grouped by local day, newest
first, each row linking to the transaction detail — the existing history rendering.
A one-line hint says planned movements live in Planned until confirmed. Empty,
loading and error/retry states are unchanged. No recurring or one-off item appears
here; a confirmed plan appears only as its real Transaction.

### Planned

A short explanation that planned movements do not change balances, spending or
analytics until confirmed. Then two clearly separated sections:

| Section | Contents | Row shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Recurring** | Unresolved (Due/Expected) occurrences of monthly rules in the month | name, Income/Expense, account, category, scheduled date, expected amount, status |
| **One-off** | Unresolved (Due/Expected) one-off planned expenses scheduled in the month | name, Expense, account, category, scheduled date, expected amount, status |

Each section has its own header link to its management page, a one-line action
hint, its own empty state, and its own error with **Retry planning**, so one failing
query does not hide the other kind. Confirmed, Skipped and Cancelled items are not
listed (as in FIN-004/005).

## Actions

| Kind | Due | Expected (Projected) | Manage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Recurring | Review & Confirm, Skip | Skip | **Manage rules** → Recurring (edit rules, restore skipped, inspect processed months) |
| One-off | Review & Confirm, Cancel | Cancel | **Manage expenses** → Planned expenses (create, edit, restore cancelled, delete) |

Review & Confirm opens the existing inline review (expected values, editable actual
amount, local date/time and note) and creates one ordinary actual Transaction.
After any action the month reloads: the item leaves Planned and, when confirmed,
appears in Actual.
